Billing

Free to join. $100 per ready buyer you claim. Paid by Zelle. There is no monthly charge and no card form on this page — nothing here charges you on its own. You pay only when you claim a buyer.

How it works

  1. Joining is free. Bge approves you. No sign-up fee, no monthly charge.
  2. Top up $500 by Zelle. Open your bank's app or the Zelle app and send $500 to the Zelle address Bge gives you one-to-one after you are approved. The address is never shown on this page — this page is public. In the payment note, write your name and "Agent Portal" — so the payment can be matched to you.
  3. Bge checks the payment, then sends you your passcode. He looks in his bank, sees the $500 arrived, adds $500 to your balance — and sends your passcode to you by phone or text. Your passcode is never written in this app. $500 buys 5 claims.
  4. Claim a buyer for $100. Each claim takes $100 off your balance. You see the buyer, the handover sheet, and the booked call time. Your balance shows on the board and on My leads, so you always know where you stand.

Two fair rules

  • 3 buyers at a time. You can hold at most 3 buyers waiting for a call. Call one and record what happened, and the slot frees up. This stops one agent holding the whole board.
  • Call within 2 days. If a buyer sits with no call recorded for 2 days, they go back on the board for another agent — and the $100 goes back to your balance. You never pay for a buyer you did not get to call.

What a claim includes

  • One ready buyer — they answered the six readiness questions and said yes to a call.
  • One buyer to one agent — when you claim a buyer, they leave the board and no other agent can claim them.
  • The handover sheet for every buyer you claim — name, phone, email, what they want, and a booked call time.
  • Your claimed leads, with outcomes, in one place.

Top up again

When your balance runs low, send another $500 by Zelle the same way — same address, same note. Bge checks it arrived and adds it to your balance.

Leaving

Stop any time — there is no monthly charge to stop, and no automatic charge. If you leave with money still in your balance, tell Bge and he sends it back to you by Zelle.
