Billing

Pay by Zelle — $499 a month. There is no card form on this page and nothing here charges you on its own. You send the payment yourself, from your bank's app or the Zelle app, to the Zelle address Bge gives you one-to-one after approval. The address is never shown on this page — this page is public.

$499 a month, per agent

One flat price, every month, for each agent on the portal. No fee per buyer, no charge per lead, no hidden extras. No fee is taken out of the $499 — the full amount lands.

What it includes

  • Ready buyers on the board — buyers who answered the six readiness questions and said yes to a call.
  • One buyer to one agent — when you claim a buyer, they leave the board and no other agent can claim them.
  • The handover sheet for every buyer you claim — name, phone, email, what they want, and a booked call time.
  • Your booked call — the buyer already agreed to the call time on the sheet.
  • Your claimed leads, with outcomes, in one place.

How to pay

Open your bank's app or the Zelle app, and send $499 to the Zelle address Bge gives you one-to-one after you are approved. The address is not on this page, on purpose — this page is public.

In the payment note, write your name and "Agent Portal" — so the payment can be matched to you.

Zelle does not charge you every month on its own. You send the $499 once a month, by the same date each month. Access is switched on by hand: Bge checks the payment arrived, then hands you your passcode. If a payment is missed, your access switches off until it arrives.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel at any time — just tell Bge and stop sending. There is no automatic charge to stop. Your access ends when the month you paid for ends. The month already paid is not refunded.
